Common
questions

What is AI Workforce?

A team of AI agents for your business. Sales AI finds and follows up with customers, Marketing AI runs your content, ads and enquiries, and Operations AI delivers the orders both of them win. A Business AI Manager oversees all three and reports results to you on one dashboard.

Who is it for?

Owners of small and growing businesses — manufacturers, distributors, service firms and retailers — who want more customers and less repetitive work without hiring a bigger team.

Will it contact customers without asking me?

Only in ways you approve. You choose which calls, emails and posts can go out automatically. Negotiations, complaints and anything sensitive are passed to you or your team.

Where does it find leads?

From permitted business directories, licensed lead databases, company websites, trade directories and industry associations. It never invents buying intent or contact details, and it respects opt-outs.

Which tools does it connect to?

Email, calendar, phone system, Google Business Profile, social media accounts and ERP. You don't need a separate CRM — one is built in, and you can import your existing customer list from a spreadsheet.

When does it launch, and what does joining get me?

Early access opens in waves. People on the waitlist get in first, and you can tell us what you sell so we can set up your agents for your industry.
