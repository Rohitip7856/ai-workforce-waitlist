Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

We collect only what we need to run the waitlist: your email, plus anything you choose to tell us. We use it to keep your place in line and to tell you about the launch. We never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • What you sell (Manufacturing, Services, Retail or Other), if you pick one after joining.
  • Who invited you, if you joined through someone's invite link, so we can credit them.
  • The date you joined, to work out your place in line.

The "See your future customers" preview runs entirely in your browser. What you type there is not sent to us or stored.

How we use it

  • To keep your place in the waitlist and count referrals.
  • To email you about early access and the launch. Launch news only — no spam.
  • To set up your AI agents for your type of business when early access opens.

Where it is stored

Waitlist data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. Only the AI Workforce team can see it. The website itself is hosted by Vercel.

Your browser also remembers that you joined (in local storage), so the page can show your place in line. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the service providers above, who store it on our behalf, or if the law requires us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data at any time, and you can unsubscribe from launch emails whenever you like. Email namansatra1016@gmail.com and we will reply within 7 days.

We keep waitlist data until the product launches or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is sooner.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
